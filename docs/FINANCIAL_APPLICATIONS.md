# Financial applications
**Insurance pricing:** premium = expected claims + risk margin + admin + profit; expected claims = frequency x severity. A Tweedie GLM on rating factors is compared with boosting on clinical features. Reported: predictive ratio (also by subgroup), R2, cohort MAPE, loss-ratio spread across risk deciles (cross-subsidy).
**IBNR:** chain ladder (development factors), Bornhuetter-Ferguson (blends paid claims with an a-priori loss ratio), and an emergence-adjusted chain ladder that rescales factors for years whose early emergence differed. The synthetic triangle has known ultimates, so errors are measured directly.
**Hospital credit:** EL = PD x LGD x EAD. Scorecards use margin, DSCR, days cash, debt/capitalisation, case mix, Medicaid share; the enhanced one adds readmissions, infections, HCAHPS, boarding and 4-quarter changes. Outputs: 4-quarter breach probability, spread per doubling of PD, implied rating band, early-warning lead time, PSI monitoring.
**Pharma equities:** rNPV = sum of probability of success x discounted cash flow, with a model-based probability. Monte Carlo over success and peak sales gives P10-P90. Patent cliffs erode 80% of revenue after expiry. A long-only mean-variance optimiser (15% cap) turns clinical-signal alpha into weights; judged by information ratio, Sharpe, drawdown, hit rate.
**ESG:** not built (no ESG data). The litigation and drug-pricing scenarios in HealthRisk Lab touch the channels only as shocks.
**Integration:** HealthRisk Lab puts the four books together so one outbreak moves claims, hospital spreads, pharma stocks and facility draws at once.
